# Company Profile: Northbank Freight Services (fictional)

> This is a fictional company created for a training exercise. No real organization, person or system is described. Any resemblance to a real company is coincidental.

## About the company
Northbank Freight Services is a mid-sized freight brokerage and warehousing company based in Memphis, Tennessee. It arranges truck shipments for customers and runs one 90,000 square foot warehouse that stores and ships customer goods.

- **Employees:** 140 (office staff, drivers' dispatchers, warehouse workers and a small sales team that travels)
- **IT team:** 2 people, an IT Manager and a System Administrator. There is no dedicated security staff.
- **Customers:** about 60 business customers, some of whom have contracts that require "reasonable security measures" and prompt breach notice.
- **Cyber insurance:** a policy renews in January 2027. The insurer has said the renewal will require multi-factor authentication for all email users and tested backups.

## Systems and data in use
| Area | What the company uses |
|---|---|
| Email and collaboration | Microsoft 365 (email, Teams, OneDrive) |
| Transport management | RouteLine TMS, a cloud service run by a vendor (99.5% uptime in its contract) |
| Warehouse management | StockWise WMS, running on an on-premises server |
| File storage | One on-premises file server |
| Backups | A network storage device (NAS) in the server room |
| Endpoints | 85 laptops and 30 handheld barcode scanners |
| Network | One firewall, office and warehouse Wi-Fi, and a permanent VPN to a partner for order exchange (EDI) |
| Payroll | Outsourced to a payroll provider with a web portal |
| Sensitive data | Driver license copies, payroll exports containing Social Security numbers, customer contracts and pricing |

## Business priorities
1. Keep the warehouse shipping. Operations say the warehouse can work manually for about 4 hours before shipments are delayed.
2. Protect customer contracts and pricing.
3. Keep the cyber insurance renewal in January 2027.

## Your role
You are the analyst asked by the leadership team to assess cybersecurity risk against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0, record risks in a risk register, and recommend a prioritized action plan. The facts you have to work with are in `assessment-evidence.md`.
